Same-version voltage correction: V[P], V[S] and cell-voltage labels/values are drawn separately at the SBMS voltage readout's 14px logical text size. Their left inset, label/value gap and right inset are equal, calculated from each full cell width and measured fragment widths. This supersedes fixed 10px visible side padding for these cells only; delta voltage and other layout/padding retain their existing behavior. Long renamed labels are ellipsized instead of shrinking the voltage. Owner requested no tests for this correction.

The Node-RED arrangement uses a 1024px-wide logical landscape canvas with uniform width-based control scale; usable height varies with system bars. The app-wide title is removed; the Ella Monitoring dashboard title is restored above the flags, consuming central gauge height only. Currents:, Temps: and Ella Monitoring have separate theme title backgrounds/outlines; the connection-status line is optional and always absent on Settings.

Every readout is a themed button with background, Labels text, Button border and press/focus highlight; gauges retain their group outline roles. Temperature roles determine a stable semantic order: internal/external ambient, charging equipment, cabinet/internal fridge, water, other channels, SBMS external, Pico battery, SBMS internal. Eight flags share a two-row box; CFET/DFET/EOC remain green On, other flags red On, Off hollow and unavailable dashed. Lock suppresses taps/long presses without stopping readings.

Central current/SOC comparisons retain the gauge style with centred faces and corrected padding. Barometer appears above LPG/water then pitch/roll. Cell voltages have their own lower bounding box, preserving previously observed channels as unavailable when stale. Time to full/empty follows the traced owner Node-RED join/calculator chain. The collector's historical capacity.timeRemaining seven-day fallback is ignored by this display; collector/MQTT/database remain unchanged.

SummaryPeriods/PopupHistory use device-local calendar starts, ending at now for Rolling or the next calendar boundary for Fixed. Monday begins each week. Hour/day/week/month use minute/hour/hour/day records respectively; week plot points group actual hourly entries into local six-hour bins. Samples counts contributing entries independently of raw record.samples and plot groups. A normal full week expects 168 entries (DST adjusts elapsed hours); month expects its number of local dates, avoiding UTC-edge inflation. Closed crossing buckets are omitted rather than prorated, so UTC daily storage/device-offset edges can leave gaps. Partial checkpoints must not include data after the selected time. Missing duration coverage prevents drawing a continuous line. Scalars/SOC are last logged values, not continuous-coverage guarantees.

Six-hour means are coverage-weighted, without zero filling missing hours. Cumulative endpoint values use actual recorded integrals and time. Calendar captions and the recorded-month dropdown use device-local labels; prior-year months append a two-digit year. Retention limits availability; monthly totals cannot recreate expired daily curves.

Raw externalLoad stays a separate selectable metric with unchanged logs. Atomic SBMS inputs may use matched non-full coverage/timestamps/sample counts, keeping gaps rather than assuming continuous recording. Incompatible/missing snapshots remain unavailable.
